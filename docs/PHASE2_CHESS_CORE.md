# Phase 2 — Chess Core\n\nAdded a strict six-field FEN parser with structural checks for rank widths, piece symbols, king count, pawn count and placement, active color, castling metadata, en-passant metadata, and move counters. Includes square lookup and FEN round-trip serialization.\n\nThis is deliberately a structural parser, not a complete chess legality engine. Full legal move validation, check detection, pins, and special-move rules still require a dedicated chess rules engine and engine-level integration.\n
